# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1. The short form:

Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people. Assume the other person is
trying to help. No harassment, no personal attacks, no publishing of other
people's private information, and no behaviour that would be out of place
in a professional setting.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and pull requests
that do not follow this, and may block repeat offenders.

To report a problem, email gagansai9090@gmail.com. Reports are handled
privately and you will hear back within a week.
